Quick start

On Linux, with mise and rustup installed. The demo replays the fee-bump and muxed-account entries through a small indexer with one planted bug; after the first fetch, nothing touches the network.

mise install
make toolchain && make fetch-replay && make build
make demo

Each entry is one rare pattern, tagged with the CAP and protocol that introduced it and the values a correct reader must decode. Hand-made entries (…) are marked synthetic. The scan of pubnet history checks every ledger, counts the shapes transactions are made of, and gives mainnet counts and first and last ledgers for the rare union arms the corpus entries hold; it runs no pipeline.

2 · Replay any indexer

Through an input it already reads, before and after the fix.

The replay harness serves the corpus to an unmodified indexer as a Galexie data lake (SEP-54) or a mock getLedgers endpoint, through a small adapter you write in any language, and reports each entry as pass, mishandled or dropped.

The reference adapter reproduces … wallet-backend incidents on their pre-fix commits and passes on the fixed ones: a signed-payload signer (CAP-40) that halted ingestion, a trustline created by the Stellar Asset Contract (CAP-73) that left no state change, and muxed and signed-payload addresses stored truncated.
