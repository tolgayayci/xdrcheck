## What it reports

A contract event's topics or data read by position or kind with no shape check and no check of the emitting
contract:

- `topics[k]` with a constant `k`, with no enclosing or earlier check that mentions `len(topics)` (directly, or
  through a variable set under such a check);
- `v.Must<Arm>()` on a topic, the data or a value read from them, with no check of `v.Type` or `v.Get<Arm>()`.

Event values are the `Topics` and `Data` fields of `xdr.ContractEventV0` and what is read from them, and
parameters named `topics` or `data` of type `xdr.ScVal`, `[]xdr.ScVal` or `xdr.ScVec`. A dominating comparison of
the event's `ContractId` with something other than `nil` fixes the shape to that contract's and silences the
report.

## Why it matters

Any contract can emit any number of topics of any type. A reader that indexes `topics[2]` or calls `MustSym()`
panics on the first event that does not have the shape it expected; a reader that stops on a panic halts the
whole pipeline on an event anyone can emit.

## Limits

The length check is matched by how the slice is written; a check made by the caller is not seen. Transaction-level
events (which only the host emits) are treated like contract events.
