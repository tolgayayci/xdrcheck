## What it reports

An operation source or an account named in an operation body (payment and path-payment destinations,
`CreateAccountOp.Destination`, claimants, trustors, the clawback source, the sponsored account, revoked
sponsorship owners) reaches a participant sink at a point not dominated by a success check
(`LedgerTransaction.Successful()`, `TransactionResult(Pair).Successful()`, or a variable holding one). A helper
that returns such accounts without checking success is silent; its unguarded call sites are reported.

## Why it matters

A failed transaction still lands in the ledger and its fee is charged, so the transaction source and the fee
source are always participants. Operation accounts are participants only when the operation was applied: a
failed payment never touched its destination. Adding them anyway lets anyone place entries in another account's
history for the price of a fee.
