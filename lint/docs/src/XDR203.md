## What it reports

An integer read from a decoded xdr value used as an allocation size (`make`, `strings.Repeat`, `bytes.Repeat`) or a
loop bound with no dominating upper-bound comparison. `len` of a decoded slice is not reported: the decoder
already materialised it, and go-xdr caps its own pre-allocation and checks lengths against the remaining input.

## Why it matters

A `u32` in a contract value or an operation is any number the submitter chose. Using it as a size lets one
transaction make the reader allocate gigabytes or spin for billions of iterations.
