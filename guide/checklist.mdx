Use this checklist to audit an indexer, explorer, wallet backend or analytics pipeline against the mistakes that [Harden your indexer against rare ledger shapes](/docs/data/indexers/build-your-own/indexer-hardening) describes. Each item links to the guide section that explains it and names a corpus entry: a ledger or vector that a reader gets wrong when the item does not hold. The corpus and the `xdrlint` static analyzer are open source and published together ([repository](https://github.com/tolgayayci/xdrcheck)). Each entry ID names a manifest, `corpus/manifest/<entry ID>.json`, that records where the entry's bytes came from and the values a correct reader produces from them; an ID without a member suffix, such as `strkey/sep-0023-v1.4.0-vectors`, names all of its members.

Items of type `linter` can also be checked in Go code by `xdrlint`, under the rule ID in the table ([rule pages](https://github.com/tolgayayci/xdrcheck/blob/main/lint/docs/README.md)). Items of type `manual` cannot be checked mechanically; the table says why and how to check them by hand. Every rule of the analyzer has an item here; not every item has a rule. For the rest of a system, see [threat modeling](/docs/build/security-docs/threat-modeling).
